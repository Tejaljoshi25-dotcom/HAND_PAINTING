# 🎨 Hand Painter | Gesture-Controlled Digital Art

**Paint in the Air with Just Your Hand! No Mouse, No Keyboard, Pure Magic! ✨**

---

## 🌟 Overview

Tired of traditional drawing tools? **AI Hand Painter** transforms your standard laptop webcam into a motion-sensing canvas! Using cutting-edge **Computer Vision** and **Real-time Hand Tracking**, this application allows you to create stunning rainbow art with simple hand gestures.

### 💡 Why This Project?
- ✅ **Zero Cost Hardware:** Works on any laptop with a webcam.
- ✅ **Touchless Interface:** Clean, hygienic, and futuristic.
- ✅ **Smart Eraser:** Pinch gesture instantly switches to eraser mode.
- ✅ **Neon Aesthetics:** Beautiful glowing lines with auto-cycling colors.
